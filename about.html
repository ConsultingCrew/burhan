About Us

Your Trusted Textile Partner

Burhan Enterprises has been bridging the gap between Pakistan's world-class textile mills and international fashion brands for over 17 years.



Our Story

Founded in 2007 in the heart of Karachi's textile district, Burhan Enterprises began with a simple mission: to connect international buyers with Pakistan's exceptional textile manufacturing capabilities.

Today, we serve clients across 15+ countries, from emerging fashion brands in the UAE to established retailers in Europe. Our annual turnover of $8-10 million reflects the trust our partners place in us.

We don't just sell fabric—we provide complete textile solutions, from sourcing and quality control to logistics and after-sales support.
